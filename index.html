Dev by day.
Founder by night.

I build web experiences that feel engineered, not assembled. Motion, performance, and clarity — every pixel earns its place.

Haven started as a Discord server for me and a few friends who couldn't focus alone. Now it's a 250-person study community with daily focus sessions, peer accountability, and a no-noise policy.

I build sites with a focus on the small things: how a button feels when you hover, how text enters when you scroll, how fast the page loads on a 4G phone. If that sounds like your kind of detail, let's talk.
